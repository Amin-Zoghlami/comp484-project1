Magnus Carlsen

Magnus Carlsen is a Norwegian chess grandmaster and undoubtedly the best from his country. He held the World Champion title from 2013-2023 having defeated former champion Vishy Anand for the title in 2013. He also holds the World Rapid and Blitz Champion titles and is the reigning Freestyle World Champion. He currently still holds the number 1 position in FIDE rankings since 2011. He also has held the highest peak rating of 2882 and holds the record for the longest unbeaten streak at an elite level in classical chess with 125 games undefeated. He is highly argued as the best chess player of all time which I would agree with.

Major Achievements

  • - Five-time World Chess Champion
  • - Peak FIDE rating of 2882, the highest in history
  • - World No. 1 since July 2011
  • - 125-game unbeaten streak in classical chess
  • - Multiple-time World Rapid and World Blitz Champion
