Robert James Fischer

Robert James Fischer was an American Chess Grandmaster and is the generally agreed upon best American Chess player in history. Some argue he was even the greatest chess player of all time though that claim is much more debatable. Regardless, he had one of the greatest peaks for a chess player ever, having swept matches against Mark Taimanov and Bent Larsen by 6-0 scores (almost unheard of). He later went on to defeat reigning world champion Boris Spassky for the title of world champion. This run from 1970 to 1972 is probably the greatest achievement in chess history. Unfortunately, he lacks the longevity when compared to the other greats like Kasparov and Carlsen. Despite that, he is my personal favorite player to analyze and is a huge inspiration for me and many others.

Major Achievements

  • - 11th World Chess Champion
  • - Defeated Boris Spassky 12½-8½ in the 1972 World Championship
  • - Defeated Mark Taimanov 6-0 in the 1971 Candidates
  • - Defeated Bent Larsen 6-0 in the 1971 Candidates
  • - Won the 1970 Palma de Mallorca Interzonal by a huge margin
