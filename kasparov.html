Garry Kasparov

Garry Kasparov is a Russian chess grandmaster and almost certainly the best Russian chess player of all time. In 1985, he beat then-champion Anatoly Karpov to gain the world champion title. He went on to hold that title for another 15 years (beating Karpov 3 other times) as well as holding the number 1 rating for a record 255 months. He had the highest peak FIDE rating of all time until being surpassed by Magnus Carlsen in 2013. When talking about longevity, this guy takes the cake. Kasparov arguably has the greatest chess career of all time and is a top contender for best chess player of all time. Many would say he is the greatest chess player of all time.

Major Achievements

  • - World Chess Champion from 1985 to 2000
  • - World No. 1 for a record 255 months
  • - Peak FIDE rating of 2851
  • - Defeated Anatoly Karpov in three World Championship matches
  • - Won the Linares tournament nine times
